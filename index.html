What is Node Factory?

An idle factory game about building AI

You place nodes on an endless grid and wire them together. Raw internet traffic becomes training data. Training data becomes tokens, insight and logic, and those become chatbots, deepfakes and trading bots. Sell anything at a Market, bank the cash in a Collector, then reinvest it in upgrades, new node slots and research.

The game has no finish line. You keep scaling, fight off malware bugs, keep the factory cool, deploy AI models for Research, and eventually go public (prestige) for AI Chips that make the next run faster.
