Input reference

Controls & shortcuts

Everything you can press or click. Menu hotkeys are ignored while Ctrl is held, so editing shortcuts never open windows by accident.

Camera

WASDPan the camera
E / QZoom in / out, toward the cursor
WheelZoom toward the cursor
Right / Middle dragPan by dragging empty space
Screen edgeEdge-scroll while dragging a node or a wire

WASD is ignored while Ctrl or Alt is held, so Ctrl+A/S/D don't move the camera.

Editing

Ctrl+ZUndo (100 steps)
Ctrl+Y · Ctrl+Shift+ZRedo
Ctrl+CCopy selection
Ctrl+VPaste at the mouse position
Ctrl+DDuplicate selection
Ctrl+ASelect all
Ctrl+GGroup selection
Ctrl+UUngroup selection
DeleteDelete selection
EscCancel the wire being drawn, otherwise deselect all

Undo covers placing, deleting and moving nodes, wires and groups.

Nodes & selection

Left-clickSelect a node or group, and show its tooltip
Ctrl+Left-clickAdd to or remove from the selection
Left drag on empty spaceBox select (hold Ctrl to add)
Left drag a nodeMove it (moves the whole selection)
Right-click a nodeContext menu: Copy, Delete, Ungroup, Collect
Right-click empty spaceContext menu: Paste
Double-click a grouped nodeRemove it from its group
Left-click a bugKill it for a Money reward

Wires

Drag from a portDraw a new wire (output → input)
Drag a wire's endDetach it and re-route
Double-click a wireDelete the wire
Double-click a portRemove all wires on that port
Double-click a bend pointStraighten the wire
Drag the Split sliderChange the A/B split ratio

Menu hotkeys

ZSkill tree (tech tree)
XMatrix
CModel Lab
PPrestige
LLeaderboard
TabSettings
EscExit menu

Matrix

Left-click a slotRandomize the item's bonus (Money)
Right-click a slotReshape the item (Research)
R / Right-click while draggingRotate the item 90°
F / Wheel while draggingFlip the item
Right-click a placed itemReturn it to your hand
Left-click a locked cellBuy the cell

Skill tree

Left-click a skillBuy it (uses the ×1 / ×10 / ×100 / Max buy mode)
Right-click a skillCenter the view on it
Drag · WheelPan · zoom the tree

Windows

Drag the headerMove the window (and bring it to front)
Double-click the headerMaximize / restore
Drag the cornerResize
Click anywhereClose an open context menu
