Resources

Every wire in your factory carries one resource. Its color and port shape tell you what it is. Only matching ports connect, and generic ports accept whole families.

Port shapes

Square

Raw inputs: Data Stream, Energy, Server Space.

Circle

Data: Training, NLP, Vision, Acoustic, Sensitive, Market, Cleaned, Labeled…

Diamond

Compute: CPU, GPU and Inference Processing.

Octagon

Refined: Tokens, Insight, Stability, Logic, Alignment…

Triangle

AI product tokens and Money.

Data (any)

Accepts any of the 9 data types.

Process (any)

Accepts CPU, GPU or Inference Processing.

Dynamic

Market, Split, Union and Researcher ports take the type of whatever connects first.

Currencies

Money ($)

Earned by selling at a Market and banking it at a Collector. It pays for rate and sell-price upgrades, Matrix cells and re-rolls, Cannon and Fan slots, and Offline Time. Lifetime Money decides your prestige reward.

Research (RP)

Generated every second by deployed models in the Model Lab. It pays for extra node slots, Processing Base and Matrix shape upgrades. Don't confuse it with the research stock, which holds the resources your Researcher banks.

AI Chips

Earned by prestige. Each chip adds +1/150 (≈0.67%) to all production rates and model rewards, permanently. You can exchange chips for $100,000 each, but that lowers the bonus.
